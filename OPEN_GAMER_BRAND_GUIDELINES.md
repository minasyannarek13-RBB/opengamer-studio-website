# OpenGamer Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0  
Status: Canonical  
Applies to: OpenGamer corporate website, service pages, portfolio pages, product pages, commercial materials, email communication, LinkedIn communication, presentations, proposals, and related brand touchpoints.

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the positioning, tone of voice, messaging, naming, claims, terminology, content hierarchy, and brand communication standards for OpenGamer.

Its purpose is to ensure that every public-facing text presents OpenGamer consistently and accurately.

This document is a canonical source.

When instructions conflict, use this priority order:

1. Confirmed Owner Decisions
2. `WEBSITE_CONTENT_HANDOFF.md`
3. `OPEN_GAMER_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`
4. `OPEN_GAMER_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`
5. `OPEN_GAMER_COMPONENT_LIBRARY.md`
6. Existing approved website copy
7. Older prototypes, drafts, brochure copy, and unconfirmed marketing text

Owner Decisions override all older wording.

- professional;
- B2B;
- specific;
- confident without hype.

- active voice;
- specific verbs;
- concrete nouns;

## 9. Writing Principles

### 9.1 Lead With What Matters

Start with:

- what OpenGamer does;
- who the service is for;
- what the result is.

Do not start with general industry commentary unless relevant.

### 9.2 Use Specific Language

Prefer:

- Slot Game Development
- RGS Development
- Game Integration
- Live Casino Development
- Dedicated Development Teams

Avoid:

- Full Solutions
- Innovative Services
- Next-Generation Offerings
- Powerful Ecosystem

unless the meaning is explained.

### 9.3 Avoid Redundancy

Do not repeat:

- full-cycle;
- technology partner;
- scalable;
- long-term partnership;

in every paragraph.

Use each term only where it adds meaning.

### 9.4 Separate Capability From Proof

A capability statement explains what OpenGamer can do.

A proof statement requires evidence.

Example:

Capability:

> OpenGamer supports aggregator and direct-operator integration.

Proof:

> OpenGamer is live with specific named partners.

Do not publish the proof version unless approved.

### 9.5 Distinguish Current, Planned, and Conceptual

Use clear status language:

- Live
- Available
- Integration Ready
- In Development
- Product Concept
- Roadmap
- Pending Confirmation

Never present a concept as a finished product.

- RGS development
- game integration
- Live Casino development
